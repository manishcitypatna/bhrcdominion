# Understanding Dermal Filler Longevity

Dermal fillers have revolutionized the world of aesthetic medicine, offering a non-surgical solution for those looking to restore volume, smooth out wrinkles, and enhance facial contours. One of the most common questions patients ask is, "How long will my results last?"

The answer isn't simple, as it depends on several factors, including the type of filler used, the area treated, and your body's unique metabolism.

## Factors That Influence Results

Several key elements determine how long your dermal fillers will stay effective:

1. **Type of Filler**: Hyaluronic acid (HA) fillers like Juvéderm and Restylane typically last between 6 to 18 months.
2. **Treatment Area**: Fillers in highly mobile areas of the face, such as the lips, tend to break down faster than those in less active areas like the cheeks or tear troughs.
3. **Metabolism**: Every individual processes filler differently. If you have a high metabolism, your body may absorb the product more quickly.

> "Consistency is key in aesthetic treatments. Regular maintenance appointments can help extend the life of your results and keep you looking refreshed."

## Popular Treatment Areas

### Cheeks and Midface
Restoring volume to the cheeks often requires more robust fillers, which can last up to two years in some cases.

### Lips and Around the Mouth
Because we use our mouths constantly for speaking and eating, lip fillers generally require touch-ups every 6 to 9 months.

### Under-Eye Area (Tear Troughs)
This delicate area typically sees long-lasting results, often extending beyond the 12-month mark.

## Maintaining Your Results

To get the most out of your treatment, we recommend:
- Staying hydrated
- Following a medical-grade skincare routine
- Protecting your skin from sun damage
- Scheduling maintenance appointments as recommended by your specialist
