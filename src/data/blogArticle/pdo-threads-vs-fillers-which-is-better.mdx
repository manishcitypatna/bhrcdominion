# PDO Threads vs. Dermal Fillers: The Ultimate Comparison

When it comes to non-surgical facial rejuvenation, two of the most popular options are PDO (Polydioxanone) threads and dermal fillers. While both aim to create a more youthful appearance, they achieve this through different mechanisms.

## What are PDO Threads?

PDO threads are medical-grade sutures that are inserted under the skin to lift and tighten sagging tissues. They also stimulate the body's natural collagen production.

- **Best for**: Lifting sagging jowls, tightening the neck, and defining the jawline.
- **Longevity**: Results can last 12 to 18 months.

## What are Dermal Fillers?

Dermal fillers are injectable substances (most commonly hyaluronic acid) used to restore lost volume and smooth out deep lines.

- **Best for**: Plumping the lips, filling hollow cheeks, and smoothing nasolabial folds.
- **Longevity**: Results typically last 6 to 18 months depending on the product.

## Key Differences

| Feature | PDO Threads | Dermal Fillers |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| Primary Goal | Lifting & Tightening | Volumizing & Smoothing |
| Mechanism | Mechanical Lift + Collagen | Volume Addition |
| Recovery | Minimal (2-3 days) | Instant |

## Can You Combine Them?

**Absolutely.** In fact, combining PDO threads with fillers is often the gold standard for a "liquid facelift." The threads provide the structural lift, while the fillers address volume loss, resulting in a comprehensive rejuvenation.

> "A holistic approach to aging often involves a combination of modalities to address both structure and volume."
