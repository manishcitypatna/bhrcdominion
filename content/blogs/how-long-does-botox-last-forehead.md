## Introduction

Botox is one of the most popular treatments for reducing forehead lines and wrinkles. But how long do the results actually last?

## How Botox Works

Botox temporarily relaxes the muscles responsible for wrinkles, resulting in smoother skin.

## Duration of Results

Typically, Botox results last:

- 3 to 4 months  
- Sometimes longer with regular treatments  

## Factors Affecting Longevity

- Metabolism  
- Muscle strength  
- Dosage  
- Frequency of treatments  

> Regular sessions can help maintain smoother and more youthful-looking skin.

## Final Thoughts

Botox is a safe and effective solution for forehead wrinkles when maintained consistently.
