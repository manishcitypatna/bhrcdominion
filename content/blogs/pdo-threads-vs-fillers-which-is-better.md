## Introduction

When it comes to non-surgical facial rejuvenation, PDO threads and dermal fillers are two popular options. But which one is right for you?

## What Are PDO Threads?

PDO threads are used to lift and tighten sagging skin. They also stimulate collagen production for long-term benefits.

## What Are Dermal Fillers?

Fillers restore lost volume and smooth wrinkles by adding fullness beneath the skin.

## Key Differences

- **PDO Threads** → lifting & tightening  
- **Fillers** → volume & contouring  

![PDO Threads vs Fillers](/images/blog/04.jpg)

## Which One Should You Choose?

The choice depends on your goals:

- Want lift → PDO Threads  
- Want volume → Fillers  

> A professional consultation helps determine the best treatment for your needs.

## Final Thoughts

Both treatments are effective, and sometimes a combination delivers the best results.
