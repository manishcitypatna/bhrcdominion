## Introduction

Choosing between PDO threads and fillers can be confusing, as both treatments offer unique benefits.

## PDO Threads

- Lift sagging skin  
- Stimulate collagen  
- Provide structural support  

## Dermal Fillers

- Add volume  
- Smooth wrinkles  
- Enhance facial contours  

## Which One is Right for You?

Your choice depends on your goals:

- Lifting → PDO Threads  
- Volume → Fillers  

![Comparison](/images/blog/04.jpg)

> Combining both treatments can sometimes deliver the best results.

## Final Thoughts

Consulting with a professional ensures you choose the right treatment for your needs.
