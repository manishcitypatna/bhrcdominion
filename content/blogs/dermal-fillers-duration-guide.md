## Introduction

Dermal fillers are a popular solution for restoring volume and enhancing facial features. Their temporary nature makes them flexible and customizable.

## Types of Fillers

Different fillers last for different durations:

- Hyaluronic acid fillers → 6–12 months  
- Calcium hydroxylapatite → 12–18 months  
- Poly-L-lactic acid → up to 2 years  

## Factors Affecting Duration

- Area treated  
- Metabolism  
- Product used  
- Lifestyle habits  

![Dermal Fillers](/images/blog/01.jpg)

## How to Maintain Results

- Schedule follow-up treatments  
- Follow aftercare instructions  
- Maintain a healthy lifestyle  

> Regular maintenance ensures consistent and natural-looking results.

## Final Thoughts

Dermal fillers offer flexibility and long-lasting enhancement when properly maintained.
